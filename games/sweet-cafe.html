SWEET CAFE

Make the orders!

  1. Customers come in. Their bubble shows what they want.
  2. Tap a customer to pick who you are serving.
  3. Build the order on the table. Ice cream: a cone or a cup, then scoops from the ice cream case, then a topping. Drinks come from the drink machine. Pastries come from the pastry case.
  4. Tap Serve. Made a mistake? Tap Start over.
  5. Customers do not like to wait! Serve them before their bar runs out.
How do you want to play?

Beat the clock: serve as many customers as you can before closing time. Each thing in an order: +10 coins. Fast service: +5 tip! For 3 stars: reach the goal, nobody leaves, and 2 mistakes or fewer.

No rush: no clock, and nobody leaves. Serve 8 customers. No mistakes gets 3 stars. No rush does not add coins. Tap Stop when you are done.
