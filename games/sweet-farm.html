SWEET FARM

Get your farm jobs done!

  1. Your farm has a to-do list. Get it all done!
  2. Pick a seed at the bottom. Tap the dirt to plant it.
  3. See a water drop? Tap the plant to water it.
  4. When a plant jumps up and down, it is ready. Tap it to pick it.
  5. Some plants grow fast and some grow slow. Each seed shows how many seconds it takes.
  6. Try not to make mistakes! A mistake is a tap that does nothing, a crop that is not on the list, or a plant that dries up.
  7. Hens lay eggs. Tap a hen with an egg to get the egg.
Pick your farmer
How do you want to play?

Beat the clock: get the whole list done before the sun goes down. Each job on the list: +10 coins. Finish early for bonus coins! For 3 stars: finish with half the time left and 2 mistakes or fewer.

No rush: there is no clock. Take your time. No mistakes gets 3 stars. No rush does not add coins. Tap Stop when you are done.

How hard?

Easy: a short list and lots of time.
